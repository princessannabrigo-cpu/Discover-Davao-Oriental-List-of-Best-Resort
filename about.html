🌴 Why we made this site

Discover Davao Oriental began with a simple idea: the first place Pacific sunrises touch the Philippines deserves more than a passing mention. We wanted people to find the everyday beauty of its beaches, forests, bays, and communities in one welcoming place.

🎯 Our mission

Make accurate, accessible tourism information easy to find—so planning a trip feels as relaxing as the trip itself.

🔭 Our vision

A province where visitors leave with deeper respect for the Mandaya culture and the natural environment.

🏝 Why resorts?

A great resort is more than a bed. It is a front-row seat to a surf break, calm bay, or quiet cove.

🧳 What makes it special

In one trip, you can explore a remarkable variety of landscapes.

  • Surf at Dahican
  • Kayak Pujada Bay
  • Trek Mt. Hamiguitan
  • Chase Aliwagwag Falls
  • Camp by the sea

🗺 A trip through the province

  1. Banaybanay — a coastal gateway from Davao City.
  2. Mati City — home to Dahican Beach and Pujada Bay.
  3. Governor Generoso — quiet shorelines and campsites.
  4. Cateel — where the coast meets waterfalls and rainforest.

🤝 Respect for the Mandaya

The Mandaya are among the indigenous peoples of Davao Oriental, and their culture is woven into the land. Ask permission before taking photos, listen to local guides, and buy from local vendors. Good travel leaves both people and place better off.
